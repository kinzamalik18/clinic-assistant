# General aftercare after treatment

This is general information. Always follow the instructions your dentist gives you at your appointment,
and call us if you are worried about anything.

## After a filling
If you had a local anaesthetic, avoid hot drinks and chewing until the numbness wears off,
which usually takes two to four hours, so that you do not burn or bite your cheek or tongue.
Some sensitivity to hot and cold for a few days is common.

## After a tooth extraction
Bite on the gauze pad for 30 to 45 minutes to help a blood clot form.
For the first 24 hours, do not rinse your mouth, smoke, drink through a straw or do heavy exercise.
After 24 hours, rinse gently with warm salt water after meals.
Call us if bleeding has not slowed after a few hours of pressure, or if pain gets worse after the third day.

## After teeth whitening
Teeth can feel sensitive for one or two days. Avoid strongly coloured food and drink such as coffee,
tea, red wine and curry for 48 hours.

## After a new crown
A temporary crown can feel slightly loose or rough. Avoid sticky foods like toffee and chewing gum
until your permanent crown is fitted. If a temporary crown comes off, keep it and call us.

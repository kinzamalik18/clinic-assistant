# About Brightside Dental and opening hours

## Location and contact details
Brightside Dental is at 14 Elm Street, Riverside, on the first floor above the pharmacy.
The front desk phone number is (555) 010-2400 and the email address is hello@brightside-dental.example.
There is free parking behind the building and the entrance has step-free access with a lift to the first floor.

## Opening hours
Monday to Thursday: 8:00 am to 6:00 pm.
Friday: 8:00 am to 4:00 pm.
Saturday: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm, for check-ups, cleanings and urgent appointments only.
Sunday and public holidays: closed. See the emergencies document for out-of-hours help.

## Our team
The clinic has three dentists: Dr. Amina Shah (general and family dentistry), Dr. Lucas Romero
(implants and oral surgery) and Dr. Hannah Cole (orthodontics and clear aligners).
Two dental hygienists handle cleanings, and the front desk team handles bookings, billing and insurance questions.

## Languages
Our team speaks English, Spanish and Urdu. Let the front desk know when you book if you would like
an appointment with a team member who speaks your language.

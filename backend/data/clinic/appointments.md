# Booking, changing and cancelling appointments

## How to book
You can book by calling the front desk on (555) 010-2400, by sending a message through the contact
form on our website, or in person at reception. Online bookings through the contact form are confirmed
by the front desk within one working day.

## Cancellation and rescheduling policy
Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move an appointment.
If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice or miss an appointment without telling us,
we charge a $40 late cancellation fee. We waive the fee for genuine emergencies.

## Running late
If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your appointment or rebook it
so that other patients are not kept waiting.

## Appointment reminders
We send a text message reminder two days before your appointment and an email reminder one day before.

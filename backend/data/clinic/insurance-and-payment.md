# Insurance and payment

## Accepted insurance plans
We are in-network with Delta Dental PPO, Cigna Dental PPO, MetLife PDP and Aetna Dental PPO.
We can also submit claims to most other PPO plans as an out-of-network provider.
We do not accept HMO or DMO dental plans.

## How insurance billing works
Bring your insurance card to your first appointment. We check your benefits before treatment
and give you an estimate of what you will owe. We send the claim to your insurer for you.
You pay your estimated share on the day, and we send a statement if the final amount is different.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, HSA and FSA cards.
We do not accept cheques.

## Payment plans
For treatment over $1,000 you can spread the cost over 6 or 12 months with no interest through
our payment plan partner, subject to approval. Ask the front desk for an application.

## Billing questions
For questions about a bill or statement, email billing@brightside-dental.example or call the front desk
and ask for the billing team. Please include your full name and date of birth, but never send card numbers by email.

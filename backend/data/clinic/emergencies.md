# Dental emergencies

## When to call emergency services
Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room straight away if you have swelling that is spreading
to your eye or neck, difficulty breathing or swallowing, heavy bleeding that will not stop,
or a face or jaw injury after an accident.

## Urgent dental problems during opening hours
Call the front desk on (555) 010-2400. We keep same-day slots for patients in severe pain,
with a knocked-out or broken tooth, or with swelling. An urgent appointment costs $95 plus any treatment.

## Out of hours
When the clinic is closed, call our out-of-hours line on (555) 010-2499.
A dentist on call will give advice by phone and can arrange to see you if needed.

## Knocked-out adult tooth
Pick the tooth up by the crown, not the root. If it is dirty, rinse it briefly in milk.
Try to put it back in the socket, or keep it in milk, and get to a dentist within one hour.

# Services and prices

Prices are for patients paying without insurance. Your insurance plan may cover part of the cost.
A dentist confirms the exact treatment and price at your appointment before any work starts.

## Check-ups and cleanings
A routine check-up with a dentist costs $75 and takes about 20 minutes.
A cleaning with a hygienist (scale and polish) costs $95 and takes about 40 minutes.
A combined check-up and cleaning appointment costs $150.
We recommend a check-up and cleaning every six months for most adults.

## X-rays
Bitewing X-rays cost $40 for a set. A full-mouth panoramic X-ray costs $110.
We only take X-rays when the dentist decides they are needed.

## Fillings
Tooth-coloured (composite) fillings cost from $140 to $260 depending on the size of the filling.

## Root canal treatment
Root canal treatment costs from $650 for a front tooth to $1,100 for a molar.
It usually needs one or two appointments. A crown is often recommended afterwards and is priced separately.

## Crowns and bridges
A porcelain crown costs $950 per tooth. Bridges are priced per unit at $900.
Crowns usually take two appointments about two weeks apart.

## Tooth extraction
A simple extraction costs $150. A surgical extraction, including most wisdom teeth, costs from $300 to $550.

## Dental implants
A single dental implant including the crown costs from $3,200. Treatment takes three to six months
from placement to the final crown. Dr. Romero offers a free 20-minute implant consultation.

## Teeth whitening
In-clinic whitening costs $380 for one 90-minute session.
A take-home whitening kit with custom trays costs $260.

## Clear aligners and braces
Clear aligner treatment costs from $3,500 to $5,500 depending on the case.
Fixed metal braces cost from $3,800. Every orthodontic treatment starts with a $90 assessment,
which is deducted from the treatment price if you go ahead.

## Children's dentistry
Check-ups for children under 16 cost $45. Fissure sealants cost $35 per tooth.

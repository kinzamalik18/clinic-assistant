# Information for new patients

## Are you accepting new patients
Yes, Brightside Dental is accepting new adult and child patients.

## First visit
Your first visit is a new patient examination that lasts about 45 minutes and costs $120.
It includes a full examination, any X-rays the dentist decides you need and a treatment plan.
A cleaning can be added on the same day if there is time.

## What to bring
Please bring photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medicines you take.
Arrive 10 minutes early to fill in the medical history form, or fill it in online from the link in your booking email.

## Children
Children under 16 must come with a parent or legal guardian. We see children from their first tooth.

## Nervous patients
If you feel anxious about dental treatment, tell us when you book. We can book a longer appointment,
explain each step before we do it, and agree a signal you can use to pause at any time.
We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for $60 per appointment. We do not offer general anaesthetic at the clinic.
